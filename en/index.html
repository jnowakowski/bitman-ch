Before our first conversation

How much does a project cost?

That depends on the goal, existing systems and agreed scope. After an initial discussion, you receive a quote. Hardware, licences and recurring costs are listed separately.

Do I need to know which technology I need?

No. Describe what you want to achieve or how a process works today. I will help you find a suitable solution and a sensible scope.

We already have an IT partner.

We can work together. I can take on a specific project and coordinate with your partner. We agree responsibilities and handover together.

Who looks after the solution afterwards?

We agree that before the project starts. You keep the access credentials and documentation. You can commission further work separately as needed.
